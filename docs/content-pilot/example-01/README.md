# Example 1: website analytics + Umami

**Local drafts for owner review. Prepared 6 November 2026 from research on 5 November. Not published or scheduled.**

Start with these two pieces:

1. [Blog: Website analytics for a small business: what to track first](blog.md)
2. [Tool: Umami: website analytics with hosted and self-hosted options](tool.md)

The blog helps a service-business owner decide what to measure and gives them a worksheet to discuss with their developer. The tool entry evaluates one possible implementation. It credits Umami's creators and distinguishes documented capabilities from hands-on experience.

The proposed voice is direct, friendly and specific. It reflects the brand brief's emphasis on understanding a problem and explaining choices. It does not invent a personal story, client result or product endorsement. Headlines and calls to action were drafted in alternatives; the selected wording is in each draft, with alternatives in the [editorial packet](research-and-review.md).
