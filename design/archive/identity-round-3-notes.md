# Lay Shah identity: approved brief and twelve sketches

Updated 28 October 2026. Status: **Stage 1 approved; Stage 2 sketches ready for owner selection**. This replaces both rejected concept rounds. No logo has been selected or applied. Open the private board at `/admin/lab/identity`.

## Confirmed brief

**Name:** Lay Shah. **Audience:** founders, businesses and small teams choosing a developer for websites, applications and practical tools.

**Foundation:** curiosity → understanding → clear communication. **Personality:** inventive and discerning. **Client feeling:** understood and confident. **Visual direction:** restraint with an unexpected, memorable character.

The owner's account is the source of this direction: curiosity, exploration and communication helped win two of their best-paying clients. The approach includes explaining what a client needs and the value of the work, helping them make an informed choice. This is qualitative discovery material, not a public revenue claim or testimonial.

The primary name stays Lay Shah. Lettering, symbol type and color are open. Website typography, palette and layout require separate review. The working creative brief is not a new public tagline. Avoid inventing personal history or symbolism from the surname.

## Findings and evaluation

- Names do much of the identifying work in this set. Josh modifies his lettering; Rauno, Paco, and Brittany largely use their names as typography within a broader composition.
- Dark backgrounds and neutral sans-serif lettering recur. They can be well executed, but using them alone would give Lay little visual distinction.
- The memorable differences come from particular choices: Josh’s displaced letter, Rauno’s spatial interruption, HOVS’s typographic contrast, and Bruno’s authored experience.
- The opportunity for Lay is a composed identity with an identifying detail that survives outside a website. The client should be able to notice that detail before reading its explanation.

- **Personal relevance:** Trace the idea to Lay’s curiosity, understanding, or communication. It should feel plausible for this person and this audience.
- **Recognizable character:** Point to one feature that can be remembered after a brief glance. Keep the name clear and the surprise intentional.
- **Legibility:** Read the name at header size and inspect the compact symbol at actual 16, 24, and 32 pixels. Test pure black and white before color.
- **Distinction:** Compare the visible work against these six identities, MUBI, and the rejected rounds. Record similarities and weaknesses without awarding intent as evidence.

Human response to character, personal fit and recall is recorded separately from automated SVG/image/HTTP checks. The owner approved the brief on 28 October: “Yes—start the 12 black-and-white sketches”. Sketch feedback and the selected pair are still pending. No memorability or final logo-quality pass is claimed.

## Stage 2: word map and twelve sketches

The approved brief led to three associations before drawing: curiosity → openings, second looks, turning a page, looking inside; understanding → listening, notes, considering another angle; communication → voices, pauses, turn-taking, sharing a finding. These are creative prompts, not a claim that every object has a personal history for Lay.

The comparison sheet contains four lettering-led, four symbol-led and four combined sketches. All geometry is authored in `design/identity-concepts.json`; the same paths drive individual SVGs, reversals, the PNG/SVG sheet and the private page. The handwritten construction is original. Other lettering starts from freely licensed outlined type studies; these are sketches, not finished custom type.

| Number | Direction | Visible idea | Main question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Open letter | The first a opens outward; a quiet detail in a direct, readable name. | The aperture needs testing at small sizes; the remaining lettering is close to a standard sans. |
| 02 | Two voices | A composed first name meets a more conversational, slanted surname. | Roman-and-italic pairing alone is easy to reproduce; selected lettering would need more authorship. |
| 03 | Unrehearsed | An upright, slightly irregular hand gives the name a candid, approachable voice. | Its informal tone may undersell complex work; the a/y junctions still need refining. |
| 04 | Margin note | Lay sits in the quiet space above Shah, like a personal note beside a larger thought. | The surname dominates and the reading order is unconventional; a header version would need testing. |
| 05 | Turned page | One surface turns outwards: exploring something, then making it available to someone else. | It can read as publishing or education before independent development. |
| 06 | Follow-up | Two deliberately long quotation forms give conversation a strong, compact rhythm. | Quotation marks are common; this risks looking like a testimonial or messaging product. |
| 07 | Listening form | An ear-like silhouette makes attentive listening the identifying gesture. | It can also read as a lightbulb, or suggest audiology; the association needs your reaction. |
| 08 | Attentive hare | A small character with one listening ear introduces warmth and an inquisitive posture. | Animal identities need repetition to become personal; this may feel too playful for your audience. |
| 09 | The aside | A folded comma holds a pause beside the name: room to listen before responding. | Punctuation can feel like an editorial studio; the fold is the detail to make recognizable. |
| 10 | Unfold | Three uneven planes open outwards, suggesting a thought being examined from more than one side. | Folded forms are common in software identities; this needs a less familiar silhouette if selected. |
| 11 | Inside out | A broad carved spiral invites a second look at what is happening inside a simple form. | The spiral can read as a G or a 6 and suggest wellness or audio; it needs a more recognizable silhouette. |
| 12 | Working note | The name lives on a folded note: a small object associated with thinking things through. | A document outline is familiar; the asymmetrical fold and lettering would need more refinement. |

**Development recommendation: 04 and 09.** The nested name gives a compact, unusual composition; the folded comma gives the name a small companion with character. The risks are real: 04 prioritizes the surname and needs a readable horizontal version; 09 uses familiar punctuation and its fold needs to become a recognizable detail. Both would need further lettering authorship. This recommendation is not an owner selection.

### Drawing and critique record

- First pass: broad name, punctuation, listening, folded-surface and character explorations. Preserved in `artifacts/identity-sketches/iteration-01.png`.
- Second pass: the first a’s cut was moved away from the y, because the first cut damaged its neighbor without opening the counter. An initial linked-diamond fold was removed because the silhouette resembled a familiar infinity/linked motif. Three unequal open planes replaced it. Preserved in `iteration-02.png`.
- Final spacing pass: increased the space between the planes and the name in 10. Saved as `iteration-03.png`. The glyphs and plates were visually checked as images, not just inspected as SVG code.
- Self-critique: 01 is close to a standard sans; 02 depends heavily on its font pairing; 03 may feel too casual; 04 has an unusual reading order. 05/06/12 draw on common editorial objects. 07 can read as a lightbulb, 08 may be too playful, 10 belongs to a crowded folded-mark category, and 11 can read as G/6. These are disclosed in the review instead of treating the rationale as evidence of originality.
- Category/library comparison: the installed logo skill’s local developer-tools catalog and searches for rabbit, comma and folds were inspected. Folded-ribbon conventions (including Buildkite/Origami examples) informed the critique of 10. RabbitMQ’s blocky frontal ears differ from 08’s curved side profile, but an animal does not become personal simply by being different. No library artwork was copied. This is a limited visual comparison, not global uniqueness evidence.
- Monochrome: inspected all twelve in black/white and reversed. Cutouts invert with the same source paths. At actual 16/24/32 px, the four symbol silhouettes remain visible; the page crease and hare’s facial cuts lose detail at 16. The lightbulb association of 07 persists. These are exploration observations, not favicon approval. A selected direction needs a purpose-built compact version.
